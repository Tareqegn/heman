About

A visionary composer and musician who traverses the realms of electronic music with finesse.

At the heart of sonicdrive's work lies a profound command of electronic music. From pulsating beats to ethereal melodies, their compositions are a testament to the boundless possibilities of synthesized sound. In an age dominated by digital production, sonicdrive stands out for their dedication to analog instruments. With a reverence for vintage synthesizers and hardware, they breathe life into their music, infusing it with warmth and character that can only be achieved through analog means.

Sonicdrive's music studio is a creative haven, blending vintage analog instruments with cutting-edge technology to produce groundbreaking soundscapes.
