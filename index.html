ABOUT HEMAN SOUND

LED BY EXECUTIVE PRODUCER KALEB BIRHANU ALONGSIDE MANAGER EBBA DANIEL, OUR COLLECTIVE BRINGS TOGETHER PASSIONATE VOCALISTS AND PROFESSIONAL INSTRUMENTALISTS.

HEMAN SOUND IS DEDICATED TO CRAFTING SOULFUL RHYTHMS, RICH INSTRUMENTATION, AND UNFORGETTABLE LIVE WORSHIP EXPERIENCES THAT RESONATE DEEPLY WITH AUDIENCES ACROSS ETHIOPIA AND BEYOND. COMBINING TRADITIONAL WARMTH WITH CONTEMPORARY STUDIO PRODUCTION, EVERY NOTE WE PLAY TELLS A STORY OF DEVOTION AND MUSICAL EXCELLENCE.

HEMAN SOUND'S RECORDING STUDIO IN ADDIS ABABA IS A CREATIVE HAVEN, BLENDING TRADITIONAL ACOUSTIC WARMTH WITH CUTTING-EDGE MULTITRACK PRODUCTION.
